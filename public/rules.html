10Block How to play

A free, open-source block-puzzle game. Drag pieces onto the grid, clear lines, build streaks.

Goal

Place pieces onto a 10 × 10 grid. Fill any complete row or column to clear it and score. There is no timer and no level — you play until no piece in the tray fits anywhere. Beat your best score.

The pieces

You are always shown three pieces in the tray below the board. The tray only refills once all three are placed — plan the order. Pieces are drawn uniformly at random; they do not rotate.

Shape families

One example shown per family (each appears in every rotation in-game).

Turn flow

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board.
  2. A drop is legal only if every cell lands on an empty, in-bounds square. Illegal drops snap back to the tray.
  3. On a legal drop the cells fill with the piece's colour and that tray slot empties.
  4. The engine immediately clears every fully filled row and column — all at once. A square shared by a full row and a full column clears once.
  5. Your score updates (see below).
  6. When all three slots are empty, the tray refills with three new pieces.
  7. If no tray piece fits anywhere, the game is over.

Scoring

1 · Placement points

+1 point per cell of the piece, every time you place it — whether or not it clears anything. A 3×3 square is +9.

2 · Line-clear bonus

Count L = rows cleared + columns cleared in that single move, then add:

Clearing a row and a column in the same move counts as L = 2 (bonus 30) automatically — there is no separate cross bonus.

3 · Combo streak multiplier

The streak counts consecutive placements that each cleared at least one line. A placement that clears nothing resets it to 0. The multiplier on the line-clear bonus is:

multiplier = 1 + 0.25 × max(0, streak − 1)

1st clearing move ×1.0, 2nd in a row ×1.25, 3rd ×1.5 … capped at ×3.0 (after 9 in a row). The multiplier applies only to the line-clear bonus — never to placement points or the perfect-clear bonus.

4 · Perfect clear

If the board is completely empty after a move, add a flat +300. The streak multiplier does not apply to it.

5 · The formula

move score = cells_placed + round( line_bonus(L) × multiplier(streak) ) + perfect_clear_bonus

Your total is the running sum of every move's score.

Worked examples

2×2 square on an empty board, no clears: 4 + 0 + 0 = 4

A 1×5 line completes a row — first clear of the game: 5 + round(10 × 1.0) + 0 = 15

Next move completes one row and one column (3 cells, L = 2, 2nd consecutive clear): 3 + round(30 × 1.25) + 0 = 41

A move clears the last cells, leaving an empty board (L = 2, streak now 5): cells + round(30 × 2.0) + 300

Game over

The game ends the moment none of the three tray pieces can be legally placed anywhere on the board (checked after every placement and refill). Empty slots — pieces you already placed — don't count. Your final score is compared to your best, which is saved on this device.

Tips

  • Look at all three pieces before placing the first — the tray won't refill until all are down.
  • Keep clearing on consecutive moves to climb the streak multiplier; one non-clearing move resets it to ×1.0.
  • A row and a column at once is worth more than two separate single clears (L = 2 → 30, vs. two ×1.0 single clears → 10 + 10).
  • Leave room for the big pieces (3×3 square, 2×3 rectangle, 5-long lines).
